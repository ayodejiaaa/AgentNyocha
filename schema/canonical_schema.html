02

Grade normalization

379 raw spellings collapse to 13 categories by detecting classification keywords (FIRST/SECOND/THIRD, UPPER/LOWER, PASS, DISTINCTION, MERIT, CREDIT) after correcting known OCR substitutions — DIVISON→DIVISION, HONORS→HONOURS, 2ND→SECOND among them. Subject-specific "Distinction in …" footnotes, common on Veterinary, Pharmacy and Physiotherapy pages, are extracted into distinction_notes rather than dropped.

03

Year normalization

Target format is YYYY/YYYY for an academic session, or a bare YYYY when the source states only a publication year. A session gap of two years is accepted as genuine — Nigerian universities have held convocations covering two sessions at once after strike or COVID disruption — 2019/2021 alone accounts for 3,437 rows.

  • Gap >2 years Treated as corruption, not a real combined session — flagged rather than accepted silently.

05

Outstanding punch list

Everything below needs a human back at the source page — none of it is safe to infer. Each is precisely located via the file and the page column, so fixing it is a lookup, not a search.

Corrupted date-serials in the year column
1,500 rows
Concentrated in the University of Ibadan files (2003, 2004, 2014, 2019, 2021). A year cell was auto‑converted by Excel into a date at entry time; the original session is gone from the spreadsheet — opening one of these cells shows things like Oct-02 or 13-May, not a session.
FixRe-derive the session from the booklet page via the row's page value — the text itself can't be recovered.
"2014/20xx" autofill cluster
84 rows, 83 distinct values
Every value starts at 2014 and ends anywhere from 2018 to 2099, each appearing exactly once — the signature of a dragged Excel fill formula rather than 83 real sessions.
FixVery likely a single constant session (probably 2014/2015) that a fill‑down corrupted. Confirm against source pages, then flat-fix the whole cluster.
Grade text leaked into the year column
28 rows
A handful of rows carry values like THIRD CLASS (HONS) or PASS DEGREE in the year field — a column-alignment slip during transcription for those specific rows.
FixRe-check against the source page; likely both grade and year need re-entry for these rows.
Reversed or implausible year pairs
133 rows, 104 distinct values
E.g. 2017/2013 (reversed) or 2006/2017 (an 11-year gap) — almost certainly a single mis-typed or mis-OCR'd digit each.
FixLow volume, spread thin — worth a quick manual pass rather than an automated rule.
